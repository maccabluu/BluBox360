# BluBox 360 public alpha roadmap

This roadmap lists current priorities, not guaranteed dates or compatibility.

## Alpha priorities

1. Improve launch reliability and preparation error messages.
2. Reduce graphical corruption and GPU load in Fable II.
3. Compare safe Vulkan driver settings across AYN and Snapdragon devices.
4. Improve controller aiming, response curves, and per-game profiles.
5. Expand compatibility reports with repeatable settings and device data.
6. Improve log sharing and diagnostics without exposing personal information.
7. Continue native multiplayer and second-controller work.

## Release approach

Public alpha builds favor stability, save safety, clear reporting, and lower
heat over high internal resolution or experimental frame-rate patches.
