# BluBox 360 0.17.0 Alpha

## BluBox Social Update

BluBox 360 now has the first Friends & Online beta on Android.

### New

- Friends & Online screen in the BluBox side menu.
- Every local BluBox profile gets its own shareable BluTag.
- Copy your BluTag from the social screen.
- Add friends by BluTag.
- Friends are kept separately for each BluBox profile.
- Remove friends from your list.
- Online presence control with Online, Away and Offline states.
- Online Beta on/off control.
- Existing profiles, saves, achievements and game library stay separate from the social data.

### Online beta status

This alpha is the first social-system test. Friend entries and presence settings are stored locally on the Android device. Internet friend syncing, remote friend requests, game invites and emulator multiplayer are not live yet. Those features need a BluBox online service/backend before they can work between separate devices.

This release does not sign into Xbox Live and does not pretend to be Microsoft's Xbox network.
