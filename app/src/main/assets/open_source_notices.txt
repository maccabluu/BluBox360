BluBox 360 0.11.0 public alpha

BluBox 360 is an independent open-source frontend around an Android ARM64,
Xenia-derived emulator core. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
distributed by Microsoft or Xbox.

The BluBox frontend is distributed under GNU GPL version 2. The complete
license is bundled at assets/licenses/BluBox-GPL-2.0.txt.

Core lineage and source:
- XenDroid: https://github.com/rfandango/XenDroid
- Xenia: https://github.com/xenia-project/xenia
- Xenia game patches: https://github.com/xenia-canary/game-patches

Xenia is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License. The complete license is
bundled at assets/licenses/xenia-BSD-3-Clause.txt. Additional third-party
credits and license links are bundled at assets/licenses.html by the emulator
core.

BluBox includes no games, encryption keys, Xbox firmware, or Microsoft code.
Use only game backups and content you are legally permitted to use.
